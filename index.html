01 / About

Curiosity, in orbit.

A computational approach to understanding how spacecraft move, navigate, and work together.

My interests sit at the intersection of astrodynamics and space systems—from the structure of multi-body trajectories to the practical challenges of satellite navigation.

I use code to explore these problems: building numerical tools, studying orbital configurations, and turning mathematical models into results that can be examined and shared.

This site brings together a few projects from my public work. Each one is a small window into the questions I enjoy working on.
